## 1. Purpose of the Document

The purpose of this document is to establish a **shared understanding of the business context, objectives, and key stakeholders** related to the insurance reporting and BI initiative.

This document serves as the **foundation for all subsequent analysis, design, and development activities**. It ensures that reporting and BI solutions are aligned with actual business needs before technical implementation begins.

This document is intentionally **business-focused** and does not contain technical or solution-level design details.

---

## 2. Business Background

The organization operates in the **insurance sector**, managing a diverse portfolio of policies and claims across multiple product lines and customer segments.

The company relies heavily on reporting to:
- monitor financial performance
- assess claims and risk exposure
- support operational and strategic decision-making
- comply with internal and external reporting expectations

Over time, reporting solutions have evolved organically across departments, resulting in a fragmented BI landscape.

---

## 3. Current Situation and Problem Statement

### 3.1 Current Situation

At present:
- Management and operational reports are sourced from multiple systems
- Similar KPIs are calculated differently across reports
- Reporting ownership and accountability are unclear
- Documentation explaining data definitions and logic is limited or outdated

Business users often rely on **manual reconciliation** or parallel Excel reports to validate numbers.

---

### 3.2 Problem Statement

The primary problem is **not a lack of data**, but a lack of:
- consistency
- transparency
- shared definitions
- trust in reported figures

As a result:
- decision-making is slowed
- discussions focus on numbers rather than insights
- reporting maintenance requires significant manual effort

---

## 4. Business Objectives

The high-level business objectives of the BI initiative are:

- Establish a **single, trusted reporting baseline** for insurance performance
- Improve **consistency and clarity** of key metrics across reports
- Enable faster, more confident **management decision-making**
- Reduce reliance on ad-hoc, manually maintained reports
- Improve transparency around data definitions and ownership

Success will be measured by:
- stakeholder alignment on KPIs
- reduced reconciliation effort
- improved usability and adoption of BI reports

---

## 5. Key Business Questions

Based on initial discussions, the following **business-level questions** were identified:

- How is overall insurance performance evolving over time?
- Which products, segments, or regions drive claims and losses?
- How do claims and policy metrics compare across business units?
- Where are potential data quality or consistency issues impacting reporting?

These questions will guide the definition of requirements and KPIs in later phases.

## 7. Initial Scope Definition

### 7.1 In Scope (Initial Phase)

- Management and operational reporting related to:
  - policies
  - claims
  - high-level financial performance
- Alignment and documentation of key business metrics
- Planning and design of BI reporting structures

---

### 7.2 Out of Scope (Initial Phase)

- Advanced predictive analytics
- Machine learning or AI-driven insights
- Real-time reporting
- External regulatory submissions

Out-of-scope items may be revisited in later phases.

---

## 8. Assumptions and Constraints

### 8.1 Assumptions

- Relevant source systems contain sufficient historical data
- Stakeholders are available for clarification and validation
- Existing BI tools (e.g., Power BI) remain in use

---

### 8.2 Constraints

- Reporting solutions must align with corporate data standards
- Changes must minimize disruption to existing users
- Data access is subject to security and compliance requirements

---

## 9. Risks and Considerations

Identified early risks include:
- unclear ownership of KPIs
- conflicting stakeholder expectations
- scope expansion without prioritization

These risks will be addressed through structured requirements management and stakeholder alignment.
